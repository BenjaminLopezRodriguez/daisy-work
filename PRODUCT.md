# Product

## Register

product

## Users

- **Requesters** — people or orgs who need work done; create Work Orders, set budget/evidence, review submissions, release payment.
- **Workers** — humans (and eventually agents) who accept assignments, submit evidence/deliverables, and build trusted history.
- **Organizations** — teams running recurring programs, policies, spending approval, and audit trails.
- **Agents** — software actors with explicit permissions that classify risk, gather requirements, recommend actions, and escalate — never exceeding authorization.

Primary context: coordinating work that ranges from simple digital tasks to regulated physical services, with payments tied to verified completion.

## Product Purpose

Daisy.work is infrastructure for assigning, governing, completing, verifying, and paying for work performed by humans or agents. The core primitive is the **Work Order**. Success means proportional governance (right verification/contracting for risk), clear evidence and acceptance criteria, and trustworthy payouts — without pretending to be a generic freelance marketplace.

## Brand Personality

Grounded · precise · accountable

Voice is calm and operational: explain what is required and why, mark mocked verification clearly, never claim autonomous legal or licensing certainty.

## Anti-references

- Generic freelance marketplace layouts (Upwork/Fiverr-style card grids as the product story)
- Purple-on-white / purple-indigo SaaS gradients
- Warm cream + terracotta “AI default” editorial kits
- Fake legal certainty badges or “AI verified license” claims
- Hero clutter: stat strips, floating promo chips, inset media cards

## Design Principles

1. **Work Order first** — every surface should make the work, its risk, and its governance legible.
2. **Proportional governance** — stronger verification as risk rises; communicate the level clearly.
3. **Show evidence, not vibes** — deliverables, credentials, and audit events are first-class UI.
4. **Agents assist, humans decide** — recommendations are labeled; final legal determinations stay human.
5. **Mocked means mocked** — seed/demo verification is always explicitly labeled.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast for body text; respect `prefers-reduced-motion`; keyboard-reachable navigation and dialogs; do not rely on color alone for risk/status.
